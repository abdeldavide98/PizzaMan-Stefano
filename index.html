The man behind the van

Meet
Stefano

Born in Egypt and drawn to the timeless craft of Italian cooking, Stefano moved to Italy to train as a chef, immersing himself in the flavours and traditions of authentic Neapolitan cuisine. What started as a passion became a calling — and that calling is now right here on your street.

Every pizza is made fresh to order, stretched by hand and fired in a traditional wood-burning oven at over 400°C. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just real Italian pizza.
